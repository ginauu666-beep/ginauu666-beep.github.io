Privacy Policy

Social Media Dashboard is a locally run application. This policy explains what it handles when you connect an account and use its features.

Data the application handles

Depending on the platform permissions you grant, the application may retrieve your account identifier, display name, profile image, channel or account statistics, public video details, analytics, and comments or messages available to your account. It stores authorization tokens and the data you collect on the computer running the dashboard.

Why it uses this data

The application uses connected account data to show metrics, track changes over time, organize available interactions, and perform an action that you start in the dashboard. It sends requests to the connected platform's official API when a feature uses that API.

Storage and sharing

By default, the dashboard's database, browser sessions, and OAuth tokens remain on your computer. The application does not operate a separate hosted server for this data. Information needed for an authorized API request is sent to the relevant platform, such as Google/YouTube or TikTok, under that platform's own privacy terms. Do not publish or share the local data directory, as it can contain account access tokens.

Your choices and deletion

You can disconnect an account and remove its locally stored information from the dashboard. You can also revoke the application's access in the platform's account settings. Removing local files does not remove content already published on a platform; manage that content on the platform itself.

Retention and security

Local account data remains until you delete it or remove the application data. The computer's owner is responsible for protecting the device and its local files. The application does not claim that locally stored data is encrypted.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the application changes. The date above identifies the current version. For questions, use the project's GitHub issues page; do not post private account data there.
